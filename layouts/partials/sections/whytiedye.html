Why Tie-Dye?

It's the piece people ask about most — and the one I have the most fun making. Here's what's behind it.

How It Started

Tie-dye was one of the first things I ever made in this studio, back before Blondie Slays was even a name. It stuck around because it's honestly just fun — messy, colourful, and never quite the same twice.

No Two Alike

Every piece is dyed by hand in small batches, so yours won't look exactly like anyone else's. That makes it a favourite for friend groups, teams, and events where everyone wants to match without looking identical.

Made To Last

Dyed, heat-set, and pressed right here in Sarnia using techniques built for wear and wash. Cold wash, hang dry, and the colour keeps its punch for years, not just a season.
